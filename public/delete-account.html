Account Deletion Request

Delete Your CaribTap Account

You can request deletion of your CaribTap account and associated personal data from within the app or by contacting support.

Option 1: Request Deletion in the App

  1. Open CaribTap and sign in to your account.
  2. Go to Profile, then Settings.
  3. Open Account settings.
  4. Select Delete Account and confirm the request.

Option 2: Request Deletion by Email

Email us at support@caribtap.com with the subject line Account Deletion Request.

Include the phone number or email associated with your account so we can verify ownership and process your request.

What Data Is Deleted

Upon successful verification, we delete your account profile and user-generated content tied to your account in accordance with our policy.

What Data May Be Retained

We may retain limited records where required for legal, fraud prevention, tax, accounting, dispute resolution, or security obligations.

Processing Time

Verified deletion requests are typically completed within 30 days.

For full details on data handling, please review our Privacy Policy.
