Frequently asked questions

Simple answers for new users, businesses, and partners.

What is CaribTap?

CaribTap is a mobile platform that helps users discover businesses, deals, rentals, services, and local opportunities across the Caribbean.

Is CaribTap only for customers?

No. CaribTap is built for both customers and businesses, including sellers, service providers, and operators who want more visibility.

Can businesses promote offers?

Yes. Businesses can publish listings and use deal or promotional tools to improve reach and customer engagement.

Where is CaribTap available?

CaribTap is built with Caribbean discovery in mind and is designed to support users and businesses across regional markets.
